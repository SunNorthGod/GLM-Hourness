## Observations that are not blockers

1. `write` in `src/data/journal.ts` calls `open(path, 'a')` outside the `try` that maps write failures to `{status: 'io', stage: 'write'}`. An `open` failure (EACCES or EPERM on a read-only file, EISDIR, a transient EBUSY from a scanner on Windows) therefore rejects `appendJournal`, although its doc comment says it never rejects for an I/O failure. The lock is still released by the outer `finally`, nothing is lost, and no caller exists yet, so I do not count it as a blocker. S4 should either catch a rejection where it wires `io` stages to row health, or move the `open` into the existing `try`. The progress note's line for S4 does not mention it; this is the place to carry it.
2. `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md` section 3 still describes the sandbox data row without `projectOverride`; `docs/DESIGN.md` 2.4 and the `dataRows` comment carry it. No action needed unless a later step edits that paragraph.
